# Plan

## Phase 0 — Join the contract spike

A landing stub and the redirect, against a development the platform. The spike itself
belongs to `the backend repository/spikes`; this repository supplies the browser side.

Gate: the browser lands on the scoped dashboard, and can come back.

## Phase 1 — Landing and signup

The public page and account creation.

Gate: a stranger can sign up and reach the login.

## Phase 2 — Login and redirect

Credentials, the second factor, and the handoff to the platform.

Gate: a client logs in and sees only their scope, with no dashboard of ours in
between.

## Phase 3 — Plan, billing, and rescue pages

Read-only account pages, plus the instructions page a notification links to.

Gate: a client can see what they pay for, and what to do when a bot needs them.
