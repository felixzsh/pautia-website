A pace designed to look after your account

Pautia avoids abrupt or repetitive sending patterns. You can adjust how it responds while keeping control of the conversation.

Your business hours

Answer during your working day or pick another schedule. Being available all day is an option, not a requirement.

Delays between messages

Set pauses before reading, replying and sending, with small variations so the timing isn't identical every time.

Conversation cues

Turn on read receipts and a typing indicator before each reply.

Varied replies

Prepare several ways to say the same thing; Pautia chooses among them without inventing the content.

Sending limits

Set how much each account can reply and how often an action can be repeated for the same person.

No unsolicited messages

The agent responds to conversations your customers started. It does not run mass campaigns on its own.

These measures help reduce risk, but no automation can guarantee that an account will never be restricted.

Honest questions

Do I need a new WhatsApp number?

No. You connect the number you already use, personal or business, and you can connect more than one on a larger plan.

What if my account asks for extra verification?

Some WhatsApp accounts require a confirmation on the phone every time a new device connects. If yours does, we tell you before you buy, and if it turns out the account cannot be automated, we tell you that too.

Can the AI send whatever it wants?

No. You review the flow and its answers before activating it. AI chooses from the options you approved; it doesn't write new replies to send to your customers.

Can I change prices or hours without anything going down?

Yes. You can edit the flow, the data and the hours in your account, try the changes and publish them. Some settings require an agent restart; Pautia tells you before you make the change.

What about my data?

Every account lives isolated from the others: what happens in one never mixes with another. And this page uses no cookies and no trackers.

Do I need to know how to code?

No. Your team builds and adjusts the flow in a visual editor. Importing chats is optional: if you authorize it, Pautia suggests a first draft; otherwise you build it from scratch with the same tools.

What if WhatsApp bans my number?

No tool can promise that won't happen. Pautia lets you set delays, reply variants and sending limits, and avoids unsolicited mass messages to help reduce the risk. How we care for your account.
