Honest questions

Do I need a new WhatsApp number?

No. You connect the number you already use, personal or business, and you can connect more than one on a larger plan.

What if my account asks for extra verification?

Some WhatsApp accounts require a confirmation on the phone every time a new device connects. If yours does, we tell you before you buy, and if it turns out the account cannot be automated, we tell you that too.

Can the AI send whatever it wants?

No. The answers are the ones we set up with you, plus the rules you set. Pautia never starts a conversation the customer didn't start, and never sends mass messages.

Can I change prices or hours without anything going down?

Yes: changes are applied live, without stopping the answers. Today we do it. You write to us, we change a price or an hour, and it keeps working.

What about my data?

Every account lives isolated from the others: what happens in one never mixes with another. And this page uses no cookies and no trackers.

Do I need to know how to code?

No. Tell us what your business needs and we set it up for you. Today we do it, and in a few months you'll be able to do it yourself.

What if WhatsApp bans my number?

No tool can promise that won't happen, and this one doesn't either. What Pautia does do is behave like a person: no mass messages, no conversations nobody started, and the pace of the replies is modulated. That greatly reduces the risk of a number being banned.
